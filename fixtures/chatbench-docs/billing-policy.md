# Billing Policy

## Invoicing

Engagement fees are invoiced monthly in arrears. Each invoice states the
engagement name, the period covered, and the fixed fee or hours billed.
Invoices for the Meridian engagement reference engagement letter Section 3.

## Late Payment

Invoices unpaid after 30 days accrue interest at 1.5% per month. The
Practice may suspend work under Section 4 of the engagement letter when an
invoice is more than 60 days overdue.

## Disbursements

Out-of-pocket disbursements (filing fees, database charges, travel) are
billed at cost with no markup and itemized on the next monthly invoice.
