RECORDS RETENTION SCHEDULE

1. PURPOSE

This schedule governs how long the Practice keeps engagement records,
including workpapers, correspondence, and issued reports. It applies to
every engagement, whatever the client or subject matter.

2. WORKPAPER RETENTION

Valuation workpapers, including the Meridian engagement file, must be
retained for seven years from the report date. Workpapers dated
2026-03-31 are scheduled for destruction review on 2033-03-31. Retention
beyond seven years requires written approval from the managing partner.

3. LEGAL HOLDS

When litigation or a regulatory inquiry is reasonably anticipated, all
destruction under this schedule stops for the affected records until the
hold is released in writing.

4. CLIENT FILES

Client acceptance records, conflict checks, and identity verification
records are kept for the life of the client relationship plus five years.
Correspondence that changes the scope of an engagement is filed with the
engagement record it modifies, not in the general correspondence file.

5. ISSUED REPORTS

Signed reports and the transmittal letters that accompanied them are kept
permanently in the report register. Draft reports are destroyed once the
final report is issued, unless a legal hold applies at that time.

6. DESTRUCTION METHOD

Paper records are cross-cut shredded on site. Electronic records are
deleted from primary storage and from backups on the next rotation cycle,
and the destruction is logged in the disposition register with the date,
the record series, and the approving partner.

7. SCHEDULE REVIEW

The managing partner reviews this schedule annually and after any change
in applicable law. Amendments take effect when published to the practice
manual, and earlier versions are retained under section 5 as issued
documents.
