# Glossary of Defined Terms

## Definitions

"Effective Date" means the date stated in Section 1 of the engagement
letter on which the engagement takes effect.

"Workpapers" means the schedules, analyses, and supporting records the
Practice prepares or obtains in performing an engagement.

"Fair Market Value" means the price at which property would change hands
between a willing buyer and a willing seller, neither being under any
compulsion and both having reasonable knowledge of relevant facts.

## Usage

Capitalized terms carry these meanings in every engagement document
unless the engagement letter defines them otherwise.
