RECORDS RETENTION SCHEDULE

1. PURPOSE

This schedule governs how long the Practice keeps engagement records,
including workpapers, correspondence, and issued reports. It applies to
every engagement, whatever the client or subject matter.

2. WORKPAPER RETENTION

Valuation workpapers, including the Meridian engagement file, must be
retained for seven years from the report date. Workpapers dated
2026-03-31 are scheduled for destruction review on 2033-03-31. Retention
beyond seven years requires written approval from the managing partner.

3. LEGAL HOLDS

When litigation or a regulatory inquiry is reasonably anticipated, all
destruction under this schedule stops for the affected records until the
hold is released in writing.
